Phase 1.4.6 • Offline
Evidence rule: Raw Original is never modified. Parsing, timezone conversion, decoding, and highlighting appear only in derived analyst views. WolfAnalyze does not decide whether activity is malicious.
What WolfAnalyze is for: structured or semi-structured security evidence such as firewall, WAF, IDS/IPS, VPN, DNS, proxy, authentication, EDR/Sysmon, web/server logs, SIEM exports, JSON, CSV, text logs, and email headers (.eml). It looks for recognizable timestamps, fields, events, headers, indicators, and security terms. Random notes, ordinary prose, or unrelated text are not logs and may not produce meaningful analysis. If WolfAnalyze cannot recognize a log structure, it will say so instead of treating every line as a security event.

Privacy Disclaimer

WolfAnalyze runs locally in your browser. It does not upload, transmit, or remotely store the input, analyst views, or reports. Nothing is recorded by WolfAnalyze on the developer end. Evidence is saved to your device only when you choose Save Report .txt or Save Report .html. Avoid saving sensitive data unless you intend to keep a local copy. Download for offline use saves a clean app copy without your evidence.
